Extended e-Invoice & e-Way Bill Login in TallyPrime

TallyPrime Features
Oct 23, 2026
4 min read
A smoother sign-in experience for GST e-Invoice and e-Way Bill work

What is Extended e-Invoice & e-Way Bill Login?

Businesses that create e-Invoices or e-Way Bills from TallyPrime need an authenticated connection to the relevant GST services. When a login session expires during busy billing or dispatch work, users may need to pause and authenticate again before continuing.

The Extended e-Invoice & e-Way Bill Login feature is designed to make that connected workflow more convenient by extending the usable login session. This can help reduce repeated sign-ins while authorised users carry out their e-Invoice and e-Way Bill activities from TallyPrime. The session remains subject to applicable service rules and may still require re-authentication when prompted.

Why it helps day-to-day operations

  • Fewer interruptions: Spend less time restarting a session during routine invoice and dispatch work.
  • One connected workflow: Continue e-Invoice and e-Way Bill tasks from the TallyPrime environment your team already uses.
  • Smoother busy periods: Reduce avoidable pauses when authorised staff are processing multiple transactions.
  • Better focus on review: Use the time saved from repeated sign-ins to check invoice and transport details before sending them.

How to use the login feature

1. Confirm your TallyPrime and GST setup

Make sure you are using a TallyPrime release that includes the feature, your company GST details are correct, and the relevant e-Invoice or e-Way Bill options are configured. Keep authorised login details available to the person responsible for the process.

2. Sign in through the available TallyPrime prompt

When you initiate an e-Invoice or e-Way Bill activity, follow the authentication steps shown in TallyPrime. Complete any required verification using the authorised account. The exact screen and prompts can depend on your installed release and current service requirements.

3. Continue your GST transaction work

Once authenticated, proceed with the required transaction and review the details before submitting them. Extended login is a convenience for the connected session; it does not remove the need to validate invoice data, GSTINs, place of supply, tax values or transport information.

4. Re-authenticate when asked

A session may expire, be interrupted, or require verification again under applicable service rules. If TallyPrime asks you to sign in again, complete the prompt rather than sharing credentials or attempting to bypass authentication.

Security reminders for your team

  • Allow only authorised staff to use the GST login for your business.
  • Never share passwords or one-time passwords in chat, email or with unauthorised persons.
  • Sign out or secure the workstation when it is unattended, especially on shared systems.
  • Review generated e-Invoices and e-Way Bills, and retain the relevant business records.

Conclusion

Extended e-Invoice & e-Way Bill Login in TallyPrime can make connected GST work less disruptive by reducing the need for repeated sign-ins. It works best alongside accurate company configuration, careful transaction review and sensible access controls. Follow the authentication prompts in your installed release and current GST service guidance.
