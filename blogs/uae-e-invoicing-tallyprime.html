UAE e-Invoicing in TallyPrime Release 7.2

Prepare, generate and review UAE e-Invoices in your TallyPrime workflow

UAE e-Invoicing support in TallyPrime

TallyPrime Release 7.2 includes workflow support to help UAE businesses prepare for e-Invoicing. It covers relevant master setup, login and registration steps, recording transaction details, generating e-Invoices individually or in bulk, and reviewing status through an e-Invoice report.

Successful e-Invoicing depends on correct company and transaction data as well as registration with the relevant UAE services. The software helps organise the process, but businesses should confirm applicable requirements and timing with official UAE guidance and their tax adviser.

Prepare your company data

Before generating e-Invoices, review the information maintained in your company, ledgers, stock items, voucher types and other relevant masters. Pay particular attention to VAT details, party Tax Registration Numbers (TRNs), units of measurement and the information required for your transactions. TallyPrime provides reports that can help configure VAT rates, map UoM to UQC, and update party TRNs in bulk.

Registration and transaction workflow

  1. Confirm you are using a TallyPrime release that supports the UAE e-Invoicing workflow and that the required company details are set up.
  2. Complete the e-Invoicing login and registration steps using the required Tally.NET and EmaraTax details. Follow the current prompts and OTP verification.
  3. Record the transaction with the relevant customer, item, VAT and other required information.
  4. Review transaction details, then generate an e-Invoice individually or use the bulk workflow where appropriate.
  5. Use the e-Invoice report to verify captured details and monitor the status of submitted e-Invoices.

Depending on the transaction and applicable requirements, e-Invoice information may also be relevant to credit notes, debit notes, point-of-sale invoices, and certain B2B transactions recorded through other voucher types. Check the current documentation for the precise cases that apply.

Useful checks before submission

  • Confirm customer and supplier party information, including TRNs where applicable.
  • Review VAT rates and transaction classifications against current rules.
  • Check item descriptions, units and quantities for consistency.
  • Resolve missing or incorrect master details before generating the e-Invoice.
  • Review the e-Invoice report and submission status; retain relevant records.

Conclusion

With TallyPrime Release 7.2, businesses can manage key UAE e-Invoicing preparation and review steps within their accounting workflow. Accurate master data, completed registration and careful review remain essential. Always refer to current UAE requirements and official Tally guidance before submitting transactions.
