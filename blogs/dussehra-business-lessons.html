Dussehra 2026: Lessons in Ethical and Resilient Business

Festival
Oct 20, 2026
4 min read
Celebrating the triumph of good, wisdom and perseverance

Happy Dussehra from Sanyam Consultants

Dussehra, also known as Vijayadashami, is a celebration of the triumph of good over evil and the courage to choose the right path. As families and communities come together on this auspicious day, it is also a timely moment for business owners to reflect on the values that help an organisation earn trust and grow sustainably.

For every business, lasting progress is built through everyday choices: keeping promises, maintaining clear accounts, learning from challenges and treating customers and colleagues fairly. Here are five Dussehra-inspired reminders for the year ahead.

Five lessons for a stronger business

1. Let integrity guide every decision

Trust is earned over time through honest communication, transparent pricing and dependable service. Make commitments your team can meet, and address mistakes openly when they happen.

2. Keep your financial records clear

Accurate, up-to-date books give you a dependable view of cash flow, outstanding payments, expenses and business performance. Set a regular schedule for reviewing transactions and reconciling accounts instead of waiting until year-end.

3. Build good habits with the right tools

Accounting software can make routine work more organised, but good processes matter just as much. Use a consistent approach to recording invoices, tracking payments, backing up company data and reviewing reports. Choose tools that suit your team's actual needs and make sure users know how to use them correctly.

4. Prepare for challenges before they arrive

Resilience comes from planning. Keep secure backups, monitor receivables and obligations, document important workflows, and review your business position regularly. Small, consistent checks can help you spot issues early and respond calmly.

5. Grow with patience and purpose

Sustainable growth is more than a bigger sales number. Set clear priorities, listen to customers, invest in your team's skills and make improvements one step at a time. A strong foundation helps your business adapt as opportunities and challenges change.

Make this festive season a fresh start

Dussehra reminds us that progress begins with thoughtful choices. This festive season, take a little time to review your books, tidy up pending records, confirm your backup routine and identify one process you can improve. Small steps taken consistently can make the year ahead more confident and manageable.

Wishing you, your family and your team a joyful Dussehra filled with happiness, prosperity and new beginnings. Happy Vijayadashami!
