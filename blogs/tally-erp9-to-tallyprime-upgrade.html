Tally.ERP 9 to TallyPrime: Upgrade, Data Migration & Checklist

TallyPrime Upgrade
Nov 17, 2026
7 min read
Plan the license, installation and company-data migration separately—and verify a backup before moving live work.

What does moving from Tally.ERP 9 to TallyPrime involve?

Moving to TallyPrime is more than installing a newer application. You need to check the license and Tally Software Services (TSS) status, install the new application, configure the eligible license, and migrate company data through the supported route for your current release. Treating these as separate steps makes it easier to plan a test run and protect day-to-day accounting.

Tally’s current help guidance says existing Tally.ERP 9 users with active TSS can upgrade to TallyPrime or TallyPrime Edit Log, depending on business requirements. The documented Tally.ERP 9 route installs TallyPrime in a new folder, reactivates the existing license and then migrates company data as required. Check the official upgrade instructions before starting.

Check eligibility and the offer terms first

Confirm your Tally.ERP 9 release, serial or license details and TSS validity with Tally or an authorised partner. Tally’s migration guidance lists valid TSS as a requirement for migrating to the latest release. Upgrade pricing and promotional eligibility can depend on the license and current terms, so request a current confirmation before committing.

About the upgrade poster

The poster on this website mentions a TSS eligibility date of 9 November 2020. That is a dated campaign criterion, not confirmation that the same free-upgrade offer is available today. Please verify current eligibility and charges before planning around an offer.

Before you migrate: a practical checklist

  • Confirm the source release: The official migration guide covers Tally.ERP 9 Release 5.0 through TallyPrime 2.1 to the latest TallyPrime release. Earlier releases may need a different, version-specific route.
  • Take a separate backup: Keep a verified copy of company data in a secure location before migration. Do not rely on a single working copy.
  • Review company data: Check that data opens correctly in the existing version and note key balances, voucher totals and GST reports for comparison after migration.
  • Check customisations: If you use TDLs or other integrations, ask your Tally partner to confirm compatibility with the target release.
  • Plan the timing: Schedule the work for a quieter period and allow time for validation before users resume live entry.

These precautions follow Tally’s data-migration best practices, which also recommend checking system readiness, GST details and customisations.

Typical Tally.ERP 9 to TallyPrime upgrade flow

1

Back up and record key figures

Create a separate company-data backup. Note the balances and reports you will use to verify the migrated company. Keep the original data unchanged until validation is complete.

2

Install TallyPrime in a new folder

Download the current installer from Tally’s official download page. For the documented Tally.ERP 9 process, install in a new folder so the older application remains available while you test.

3

Configure the existing license

In TallyPrime, choose Reactivate Existing License, sign in with the Tally.NET ID and password, select the required Tally.ERP 9 license and confirm activation. Follow the current on-screen instructions if they differ for your license.

4

Migrate a company using the supported path

For supported source releases, open TallyPrime and go to F1 (Help) > Troubleshooting > Migrate, or Alt+Y (Data) > Migrate. Select the company, choose the backup and migration options shown, and proceed according to the official Tally.ERP 9 migration instructions.

5

Review the migration summary before going live

Tally’s documented migration creates a new six-digit company and retains the original five-digit company for access in the earlier release. Review the Migration Summary Report, compare key balances and GST reports, and test customisations before relying on the migrated company for live work.

Common questions

Will my original Tally.ERP 9 data be removed?

The documented migration route retains the original company and migrates data to a new company. Still, make and verify a separate backup first, and follow the steps for your exact source and target releases.

Can I keep using Tally.ERP 9 during the migration?

Tally’s migration guidance describes an option to continue working in the previous compatible version while migration is in progress. Availability depends on the migration route and settings; do not assume it applies to every version or setup.

Is the upgrade free?

Do not rely on an old poster or a past campaign date for current pricing. Check TSS validity and ask Tally or an authorised partner to confirm the current upgrade terms for your license.
